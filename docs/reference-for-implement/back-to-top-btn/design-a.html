Design A — "The Dot" · scroll to see the button arrive

Back-to-top, quoting the mark

The button doesn't invent a new visual language — it borrows the one thing the logo already owns: a small blue dot, held inside a charcoal ring. Scroll down to bring it in.

1One entrance, not a fade

Past a scroll threshold the button pops in once, with a short overshoot, instead of fading in on every scroll tick. It only ever announces itself once per visit to the bottom of the page.

2The ring is a meter

The blue arc isn't decorative — its length is how far down the page you are. Watch it while you scroll; it empties again as the page returns to the top.

3A wink, not a bounce

Hovering plays a quick pinch on the dot — a literal wink, since that dot is the same shape as the counter of the "9". No generic bounce or bright glow.

4Long content, for testing

Scroll further to confirm the button stays anchored, the ring keeps tracking, and nothing about the animation feels different the fifth time as the first.
